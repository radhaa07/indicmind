Enterprise Security & Privacy Architecture

How IndicMind safeguards your sensitive legal agreements, NDAs, and confidential data.

Zero Permanent Raw File Retention

Active Policy

When you upload a PDF, DOCX, TXT, or image document (JPG, PNG) to IndicMind, the document is streamed directly into temporary in-memory buffers for text extraction and semantic chunking. The physical file is immediately purged from the server filesystem upon extraction.

✓ Instant File Deletion: Uploaded temporary files are unlinked from disk before analysis completes.
✓ Ephemeral Processing: Only normalized clause vectors and user-saved reports persist in your private account database.

Strict Non-Training Guarantee

Guaranteed

Your agreements are confidential trade secrets. IndicMind utilizes enterprise API tiers with Google Cloud Vertex/Gemini and local Ollama daemons where customer data is strictly barred from training public foundation models.

Airgapped & 100% Offline Mode

Supported

For organizations dealing with highly restricted documents (ITAR, classified, or strict GDPR constraints), IndicMind supports 100% local execution via Ollama and ArgosTranslate. When in offline mode, no data ever leaves your internal server or laptop.

Privacy Policy & Legal Disclaimers

1. Information We Collect

We collect your account email address and name strictly for authentication (delivering 6-digit OTP codes) and saving your document analysis reports.

2. Not Legal Advice

IndicMind is an artificial intelligence-assisted productivity tool designed to accelerate document due diligence. Outputs do not constitute formal legal representation or attorney-client privileged advice. Always have qualified legal counsel review final documents.
