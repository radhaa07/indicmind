What is NER? Named Entity Recognition detects and classifies key entities — persons, organizations, locations, dates, amounts — turning unstructured text into structured data. As noted in the Medium article, it powers information extraction, search, chatbots, knowledge graphs and RAG systems by helping LLMs know who, what, where and when in a document.
